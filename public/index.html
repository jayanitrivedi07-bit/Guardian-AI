Honest Transparency

Never wonder if your circle saw your alert.

Most apps show a misleading checkmark even when offline. Guardian AI provides true provider delivery status:

  • Queued: Stored locally on phone; waiting for cellular signal.
  • Dispatched: Submitted to network gateway for transmission.
  • Delivered: Confirmed delivery packet received on contact's phone.
  • Acknowledged: Contact tapped and opened the emergency notification.
